# Samer Bou Nassif, CMA

I work in finance and business transformation, CFO advisory, and reporting. Over 8 years at Deloitte and in independent consulting, I have worked on finance operating models, process and control design, FP&A, IFRS reporting, and sustainability reporting across Qatar and the wider region.

I build practical Python prototypes to explore how finance teams can validate data, analyse performance, and produce clearer reports. The public projects below use mock or sample data and are independent portfolio work, not client deliverables or production systems.

## Selected projects

- **[FP&A Reporting & Forecasting Tool](https://github.com/samer-bn/fpna-reporting-tool)** — Validates sample financial data, analyses budget variances, produces a management report, and builds a simplified driver-based forecast.
- **[ESG Reporting Workflow](https://github.com/samer-bn/esg-reporting-workflow)** — Cleans and checks mock ESG data, analyses metrics, and creates an Excel reporting output.

Based in Doha, Qatar, and open to relocation for finance transformation and digital finance opportunities.
